RIGHT  PLACE  ·  RIGHT  SOUND  ·  RIGHT  PEOPLE

"This isn't a party brand. This is a collective. A culture. A sonic sanctuary."

Nivaant Sessions was never built for the mainstream. It was born out of restlessness — a need to carve space for sounds that don't fit algorithms, for artists who don't chase trends, and for music that demands to be felt, not consumed.

What began as underground radio transmissions on Boxout.fm and Hanoi Community Radio became a movement — a frequency connecting cities, scenes, and selectors. From intimate studio sessions to YouTube archives, we've hosted 50+ guest artists, including Kratex, Nanku, Harlin Beats, Mohit, and many others shaping the pulse of the underground.

We don't book artists for hype. We platform them for honesty. Now, Nivaant Sessions steps beyond the booth — intentional gatherings, sound systems tuned for depth, spaces chosen for energy, nature as the backdrop, community as the core.
